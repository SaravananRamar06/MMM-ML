What the comparison shows

The network does the predicting; the equation gives it names

  • The JC-PINN is about times more accurate than Equation 1 alone on the test cases. Equation 1 is a product of separate terms, so it gives every condition the same curve shape, and the simulations change shape with temperature and rate.
  • The same network trained with Equation 1 switched off is better on every measure. The gain over the earlier MLP on unseen conditions comes from the training recipe (strong weight decay, log-space output), not from the equation.
  • None of the 13 settings that pulled the model toward Equation 1 (level or derivative penalties) improved the training-only score, so the selected model uses the equation as structure only.
  • The porosity exponent r settles at 0: density changes stress by only about 0.2 % in these simulations.
  • Above 200 °C there is no data and no physics penalty, so those curves are unvalidated extrapolation.
