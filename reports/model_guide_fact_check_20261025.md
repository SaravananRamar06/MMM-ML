# Required corrections to the model guide draft

Codex review, 25 October 2026. Draft `model_guide.tex` / `.md` reviewed at 12:42 UTC. Not ready for delivery. Gemini retains edit ownership.

1. **Architecture:** actual final model is five independently initialized **5–16–16–1 tanh** networks, not 64–64/ReLU. Each has 385 weights/biases, ensemble 1,925. Include averaging and target inverse scaling in the equation. Output of each estimator is standardized stress before inverse scaling.
2. **Input order and transformation:** `[T, log10(rate), density, G, E11]` inside the network. External `.predict(X)` takes shape `(n_cases, 4)` with `[T, log10(rate), density, G]` and returns `(n_cases, 120)`. Raw rate is not used without logarithmic encoding.
3. **Units/mapping:** G is supplied numerically as 23313.95–25300 (presumably MPa), density 2488.05–2700 (presumably kg/m³), not 27 GPa/2.68 g/cm³ in raw inference. Units remain unconfirmed. Neither G nor density is an established porosity proxy. Do not claim a porosity mapping.
4. **Dataset/training:** 73 available histories, only 36 used for fitting and 37 for evaluation. Training operates on **4,320 query samples** (36 x 120), evaluation 4,440, with equal case contribution. Invented train/test raw-row counts must be replaced by manifest sums or omitted. Original rows are not independent training examples.
5. **Evaluation disclosure:** explicitly disclose earlier full-data exploration/selection influence. A disjoint fitting partition does not establish an untouched holdout. Remove “zero data leakage” / “completely blind” framing.
6. **Cache verification scope:** zero difference means the cached **120 interpolated stress samples per curve** agree with raw-file interpolation and source hashes/row counts match. It does not mean lossless preservation of all raw observations. Original files are retained separately.
7. **Case 60:** its generating cause is unknown. Do not attribute it to solver convergence or claim it is not model error. Recorded Evm reverses while E11 increases; retain and flag it. High CV error at this case is real evidence of limited generalization.
8. **Loading scope:** empirical response along the supplied loading family. Remove “any trajectory”, verified plastic-strain/flow-law claims, definitive constitutive-input/FEA validation claims, and assertions that source solver is established. No bracket or aerodynamic coupling has been validated.
9. **Runtime example must run:** use `QueryRuntime.load('runs/score/final_Svm/model_numpy.npz')`, `X = np.array([[100.0, -4.0, 2619.0, 24541.0]])`, `curve = runtime.predict(X)[0]`. There is **no `predict_point` method**, and `runs/query_mlp_weights.npz` does not exist. Query strain grid is `runtime.strain`; `np.interp(0.05, runtime.strain, curve)` obtains a point from the curve. Explain fixed 120-point delivered interface versus possible continuous MLP formulation.
10. **Runtime caveats:** original stack uses sklearn, not PyTorch. Timings include fresh interpreter/import/load/one curve; are local repeated measurements, not universal. NumPy was slower at batch 73 (21.8 vs 17.3 ms); include that. Adapter is not yet integrated into Studio.
11. **Condition score labels:** 23.52512868 is mean RMSE for the held-out hot/slow condition, not a universal maximum point error. Condition-holdout mean 6.79360255 averages condition means equally; CV/test mean average cases. Do not conflate them.
12. **Unsupported comparisons/claims:** verify the baseline 1.38 / 23x claim against a same-target/same-split saved result or omit. Remove “ironclad”, “mathematically sound”, and “reliable engineering workflow” claims beyond reproducibility evidence. Explain what RMSE means and why 0.059 test vs 6.79 condition error differ.
13. **Complete accessible explanation:** add a worked prediction example, training steps (standardization, target scaling, L-BFGS, alpha 0.001, five seeds, train-only CV), RMSE definition, how the ensemble average works, known unanswered physics questions, reproducible commands, evidence paths and plain-language viva Q&A.
14. **Formatting:** compile LaTeX to a readable PDF and visually inspect all pages. Source/Markdown alone do not finish the requested well-formatted report.
