    Definitions needed for physical predictions

      1. Material response versus porosity

      Density is converted using ρ = ρsolid(1 − φ). This requires bulk density and a justified solid reference. Supply the shear-modulus relation separately. An assumed mapping produces an exploratory material study.

        2. Compare supplied bracket and aerodynamic results

        Import the results from solved cases. Displacement and stress are read from your solver export. Lift coefficient is calculated only when lift force, air density, speed and reference area are supplied together.

        Required columns and comparison rules

        Use consistent N, mm, MPa and °C units for structural results. The aerodynamic fields use N, kg/m³, m/s and m². An independent group identifies related runs that must stay together in future model evaluation. A comparison group identifies a controlled study where only the intended material/porosity variables change. Keep geometry, loads and flow conditions consistent when comparing porosity cases.

        A single zero-porosity case is required in each matched group to calculate changes. Importing results does not verify mesh convergence, the material law or the structural-to-aerodynamic coupling.
